## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a cutting-edge pharmaceutical and peptide biotechnology identity. It synthesizes institutional scientific authority with modern digital-health commercial precision. The aesthetic balances deep-tech computational biology with clinical purity: rich midnight navy surfaces, luminous crisp-white clinical card decks, cold electric-cyan accents, and molecular emerald bio-signals.

The visual direction marries **Corporate Modernism** with **Controlled Glassmorphism**. Layouts convey scientific rigor through micro-mesh grid structures, high-precision typographic hierarchy, and subtle frosted overlays for credentials, clinical trial markers, and patent tags. It avoids generic clinical sterility by incorporating high-contrast luminescent focal points, disciplined depth layering, and tactile B2B/e-commerce purchasing paths for proprietary formulations (such as Allomedin PRO, Entomix, Allostatin, and FLIP7).

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative biopharmaceutical ecosystem:

- **Primary (`#243D83`)**: Deep Clinical Cobalt. Serves as the bedrock tone for corporate presence, main headers, primary interactive surfaces, and key brand framing.
- **Secondary (`#00D2FF`)**: Bio-Electric Cyan. High-energy luminescence used for active states, molecular focus nodes, dynamic hover highlights, and innovation badges.
- **Tertiary (`#00C389`)**: Bio-Synthesis Emerald. Communicates efficacy, organic peptide validation, in-stock availability, and clinical validation metrics.
- **Neutral (`#0B132B`)**: Deep Obsidian Navy. Replaces standard flat grays with an ultra-dense navy tone for supreme typographic contrast, deep dark-mode hero canvases, and robust baseline texts.
- **Clinical Red (`#E51B24`)**: Preserved from historic brand heritage as an accent for prescription warnings, high-priority trial callouts, and urgent clinical indications.
- **Soft Ice (`#E2E9F3`)**: Background neutral tint providing cool, clinical separation across content tiers without introducing drab warmth.

Surfaces use high-purity `#FFFFFF` containers suspended over `#E2E9F3` or layered atop `#060A17` dark-mode hero canvases to evoke cleanroom conditions and computational labs.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy relies on a three-tier technical font pairing:

1. **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, structured, and modern. Its geometric proportion lends cutting-edge scientific weight to product nomenclature and value propositions.
2. **Body Text (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Ultra-clean, contemporary sans-serif engineered for effortless scanning across long clinical abstracts, pharmacological compositions, and dosage specifications.
3. **Data & Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Monospaced utility font applied to chemical formulas, batch numbers, clinical trial identifiers, molecular weights, and price tags.

Letter spacing is tightly calibrated: large display headers maintain subtle negative tracking (`-0.02em`) to enhance structural cohesion, while mono badges use expanded tracking (`+0.05em`) for computational clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on an 8pt modular grid structured inside a 12-column responsive layout.

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns with 32px (`2rem`) gutters and a max canvas width of 1360px centered with dynamic page margins (`5rem`). Product showcase grids distribute in balanced 3- or 4-column tiers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Dual-product comparisons reflow horizontally.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px page margins. High-density metrics, product dosage tiers, and CTA stacks shift into single-column vertical flows.

Vertical cadence employs alternating generous structural margins between functional scientific modules, offset by compact, information-dense spacing within data tables, spec sheets, and product configuration panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphism anchored by sharp, clinical ambient drop shadows:

- **Surface 0 (Canvas Base)**: Cool tint `#E2E9F3` in clinical sections; Deep Midnight `#060A17` in dynamic hero modules and mechanism-of-action visualizers.
- **Surface 1 (Primary Laboratory Cards)**: Solid `#FFFFFF` containers framed with a crisp hairline border (`1px solid rgba(36, 61, 131, 0.08)`) and ambient diffusion (`box-shadow: 0 8px 32px -4px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.06)`).
- **Surface 2 (Floating Interactivity & Badges)**: Translucent glass layers (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78); backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with crisp internal glow borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), used for hover states, floating shopping bags, sticky consultation headers, and clinical certification indicators.
- **Surface 3 (Overlays & Dialogs)**: Modals and deep formula inspection panels lift through deep multi-layer ambient occlusions (`box-shadow: 0 24px 64px -12px rgba(6, 10, 23, 0.28)`), layered over backdrop dimmers with 20px Gaussian blur.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape standard:

- Standard interactive controls, text inputs, and micro-cards use `8px` (`0.5rem`) radii.
- Large product showcases, clinical study containers, and imagery viewports use `16px` (`1rem`) corner radiuses (`rounded-lg`).
- Feature hero cards and focal interaction blocks use `24px` (`1.5rem`) radiuses (`rounded-xl`).
- Micro-tags, molecular property pills, status beacons, and trial phase pills utilize fully rounded caps (`pill-shaped` / `9999px`) to visually separate metadata from structural content modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: `#243D83` solid background, white text, 8px border radius, with a subtle electric cyan border hover transition and dynamic shadow (`0 4px 16px rgba(36, 61, 131, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Scientific**: Frosted surface with hairline border (`rgba(36, 61, 131, 0.2)`), `#243D83` text, lifting slightly on hover with `#E2E9F3` fill.
- **High-Conversion B2B Action**: Electric Cyan (`#00D2FF`) base with deep navy `#0B132B` bold text for immediate commercial conversion in wholesale, partner portal, and retail purchasing triggers.

### Product Showcase Cards (Allomedin PRO, Entomix, Allostatin, FLIP7)
- Rigid white surface with a multi-layered structure: top status pill indicating pharmacological class (e.g., *Antiviral Peptide*, *Innate Immunity*), high-definition 3D product render viewport, title in `Space Grotesk`, formula code in `JetBrains Mono`, concise clinical indication summary, and instant B2B/E-commerce checkout strip with quantity selectors and tier-pricing tooltips.

### Chips & Clinical Badges
- Semi-translucent capsules (`padding: 4px 12px; font-size: 11px; font-family: JetBrains Mono; font-weight: 500`).
- Color coded: Cyan for innovation/patents (`bg-cyan-500/10 text-cyan-600 border border-cyan-500/20`), Emerald for validation/stock (`bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 border border-emerald-500/20`), Red for clinical warnings.

### Form Inputs & Order Modals
- High-contrast clinical inputs with `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid rgba(36, 61, 131, 0.16)` border, switching to electric cyan glow focus rings (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 210, 255, 0.25)`). Accompanying micro-labels positioned above inputs in uppercase technical monospaced styling.

### Data Tables & Molecular Spec Sheets
- Alternating row styling using `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`, hairline horizontal dividers (`rgba(36, 61, 131, 0.06)`), with numerical parameters rendered in `JetBrains Mono` for maximum scannability.
